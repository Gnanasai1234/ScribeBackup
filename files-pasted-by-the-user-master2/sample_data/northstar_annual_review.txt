NORTHSTAR REGIONAL TRANSIT — ANNUAL SERVICE REVIEW
Planning year 2025 · Public planning office · Demo source

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Northstar Regional Transit carried 8.4 million passenger trips in 2025, an increase of 6.2 percent compared with the previous year. Weekend ridership grew faster than weekday ridership. The board approved a two-year pilot to extend evening service on the Green and Harbor routes, subject to quarterly review.

SERVICE PERFORMANCE
The Green route recorded the largest increase in passenger trips, rising by 11 percent after headways were shortened during peak periods. The Harbor route carried 1.9 million passengers. On-time performance across the network was 84 percent, below the agency target of 90 percent. The review attributes missed trips to vehicle availability and operator vacancies, but does not quantify the share attributable to each factor.

FINANCIAL POSITION
Fare revenue was $31.6 million, while operating costs totaled $78.2 million. A regional mobility grant provided $12 million for fleet upgrades. The finance office estimates that the evening-service pilot will cost between $2.1 million and $2.6 million over two years. This estimate excludes any future fuel-price changes.

RISKS AND LIMITATIONS
The service review identifies operator vacancies as a risk to reliable service. It also notes that the pilot cost estimate depends on staffing and fuel assumptions. The report does not provide route-level emissions data. The ridership increase may partly reflect a change in the counting method introduced in March, so year-over-year comparisons should be interpreted cautiously.

BOARD DECISIONS
The board approved the evening-service pilot for the Green and Harbor routes for two years. The board requested a progress report every quarter. No decision was recorded on expanding service to other routes.

RECOMMENDATIONS
The planning office recommends tracking on-time performance, missed trips, staffing levels, and passenger counts each quarter. It recommends publishing a separate note on the March counting-method change before the next annual comparison. The review does not assign an owner or deadline for these recommendations.

ACTION ITEMS
The board requested quarterly progress reporting for the pilot. The source does not name a responsible owner or specify calendar due dates.
